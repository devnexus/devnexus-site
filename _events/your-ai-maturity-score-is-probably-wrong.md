title: Your AI Maturity Score Is Probably Wrong
description: "Your organization has AI coding tools. Your developers use them every
  day. You may have an AI strategy, a governance program, and a growing list of AI
  projects. Does this make your organization AI mature?\r\nMost organizations measure
  what is easy to count: licenses, adoption rates, generated code, and reported hours
  saved. These metrics show that developers use AI. They do not show that the organization
  builds better software with AI. \r\nThis talk compares the major AI maturity models
  from CMU/SEI, DORA, NIST, and OWASP. We will show what each model measures, where
  the models overlap, and where they fail. Then we will discuss a practical framework
  that answers three questions:\r\nAdoption: Do we use AI?\r\nCapability: Do we have
  the engineering practices to use AI well?\r\nOutcomes: Can we prove that AI improves
  delivery speed, quality, security, or developer experience?\r\nYou will see why
  high adoption often hides low maturity, why individual productivity metrics mislead
  leaders, and what to measure instead. The goal isn’t another five-level AI maturity
  staircase. It’s a way to gather evidence that an organization is actually getting
  better at building software in an AI-enabled world.\r\n"
