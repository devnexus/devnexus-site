title: 'Autopilot: Building the Ultimate Ops Engineer with JFR and AI'
description: "JDK Flight Recorder (JFR) is an observability and diagnostic framework
  built directly into the JDK. It can provide a rich and detailed view into how your
  application and the JVM are behaving at runtime. It is able to do this while maintaining
  a sub-1% impact on application throughput, allowing it to be always on in production.\r\n\r\nWhile
  JFR is an incredibly powerful tool, its utilization is rather low, and the amount
  of information it provides can feel overwhelming. JFR can be always on, but the
  engineers needed to analyze its recordings can't be. And if an issue happens at
  2:00 a.m., it might mean waiting hours before a human engineer can review the recording
  and offer a solution.\r\n\r\nIn this presentation, we will look at how JFR can be
  combined with agentic AI to build the ultimate Ops engineer: an engineer that can
  review JFR recordings and propose improvements to an application; an engineer that
  is always available, day and night, and able to respond in real time as issues occur.\r\n\r\nIf
  you have been looking for novel opportunities to incorporate AI into your organization
  or improve the performance of your applications in production, this is a presentation
  you don't want to miss.\r\n"
