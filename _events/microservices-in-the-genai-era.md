title: Microservices in the GenAI Era
description: "The microservice architecture has traversed the hype cycle and is no
  longer as popular as it once was.\r\nIn part, there's been an unhealthy backlash
  to the hype and to poorly designed microservices.\r\nBut mostly, the industry has
  matured: organizations have learned to use the microservice architecture only when
  they suffer from the socio-technical problems it addresses.\r\nToday, however, a
  new technology — also in the middle of its own hype cycle — is dramatically increasing
  the need for microservices: GenAI coding agents.\r\n\r\nThis two-part talk explores
  the surprising connection between GenAI and microservices.\r\nIn the first part,
  I explain why GenAI coding agents, by scaling software development, amplify the
  rate of change and create the very scaling pressures that the microservice architecture
  is designed to handle.\r\nYou will learn how to recognize the signals that indicate
  GenAI coding agents have caused you to outgrow your monolith.\r\nIn the second part,
  I show how GenAI can help you design a microservice architecture.\r\nI demonstrate
  an AI-based assistant that guides you through the process.\r\nYou will learn how
  GenAI can accelerate the journey from monolith to a well-designed microservice architecture."
