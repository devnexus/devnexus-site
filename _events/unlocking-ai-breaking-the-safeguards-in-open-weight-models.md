title: 'Unlocking AI: Breaking the Safeguards in Open-Weight Models'
description: "Model creators bake safety alignment into open weight models because
  they do not want to release a personalized Anarchist Cookbook, creating foreseeable
  harm, public backlash, and legal exposure. Many users want something different:
  capable models with fewer refusals, fewer restrictions, and less control retained
  by the developer.\r\n\r\nOnce the weights are downloaded, who gets the final say?\r\n\r\nThis
  talk follows the craft of model lockpicking. Researchers have shown that refusal
  behavior is not an unchangeable property of a model. It is produced by internal
  mechanisms that can be identified, weakened, and sometimes removed while much of
  the capability remains intact.\r\n\r\nWe will explore the latest research in modifying
  models to provide less push back or refusals for safety-aligned prompts and what
  the trade-offs are between refusal reduction and model accuracy.\r\n\r\nThis is
  not another prompt jailbreaking talk. Prompt attacks try to persuade a model to
  bend its rules. Model lockpicking changes the model itself.\r\n\r\nCome see how
  we in the model-cracking community play a cat and mouse game with open weight model
  makers today, and why safety aligned may describe only the model as it left the
  factory."
