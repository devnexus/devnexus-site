title: Pouring AI Sauce on Your Architecture
description: "Today, you are being besieged by people claiming that AI is the secret
  ingredient that will solve every problem in your company. But AI is the sauce, not
  the meal—don’t let it drown your systems. There is tremendous business pressure
  to introduce AI into all aspects of your organization; however, many stakeholders,
  division leads, and developers lack a consistent understanding of what that truly
  means or a unified approach for assembling these pieces into a coherent system.\r\nAs
  architects, we must navigate the pros and cons to create architectures that solve
  business needs both today and in the future. We must also guide each of these groups
  toward building effective solutions. AI impacts your entire software development
  lifecycle, altering how you design, build, and test systems. As an architect, you
  need to design systems that take advantage of AI while limiting the blast radius
  of its approximations.  In an AI world, the fundamentals of software engineering,
  the practices, the discipline, the ceremonies, are *more* critical today as AI enables
  us to move faster with higher quality.\r\n"
