title: Fundamentals of Software Engineering in the Age of AI
description: "AI doesn't replace what you know. It multiplies it. Hand a coding agent
  to someone who understands design, testing, and how systems fail, and they move
  faster than ever. Hand it to someone who doesn't, and they produce more code they
  can't explain, faster than ever. Same tool. Very different results.\r\n\r\nThat's
  what the \"software engineering is dead\" headlines miss. We've heard it before
  with code generators, low-code, and no-code. Each time, the job changed, the fundamentals
  stayed, and the people who understood the craft came out ahead.\r\n\r\nIn this keynote,
  Dan Vega and Nate Schutta look at what is really changing in our work and what is
  just noise. We'll talk about the skills that matter more now, not less: reading
  code critically, naming and design, testing, and the judgment to know when an answer
  is wrong. You'll leave knowing where to invest your time, and why the craft is worth
  more than ever."
