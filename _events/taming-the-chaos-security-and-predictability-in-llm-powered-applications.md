title: 'Taming the Chaos: Security and Predictability in LLM-Powered Applications'
description: "LLMs are probabilistic by nature. Ask the same question twice, get different
  answers. Great for creativity, terrible for business logic. How do you build reliable
  systems on top of something inherently unreliable?\r\n\r\nLet's tackle this together
  – from security to predictability. We'll look at guardrails that block prompt injection
  and redact leaked PII before it leaves your system. Then we'll layer on deterministic
  validation that catches hallucinations with code, not hope. And when the model gets
  it wrong, we'll feed errors back for self-correction and use multi-model consensus
  to build confidence. Practical patterns, real code, no magic.\r\n\r\nThis isn't
  about making LLMs perfect – it's about building systems that fail safely and predictably
  when they're not.\r\n\r\nThe examples are in Java and LangChain4J; the concepts
  – transferable to other languages and frameworks."
