title: 'Making the JVM Faster with AI: Promises and Pitfalls'
description: "AI is radically changing the art of software development.  The most
  common use cases involve enhanced IDE autocompletion, tools like Claude Code and
  even the idea of Vibe coding entire applications.  This is, of course, targeted
  at traditional end-user or B2B type applications.  To date, AI has not been used
  much in lower-level systems programming and core infrastructure; in fact, OpenJDK
  explicitly bans the submission of AI-generated code.\r\n\r\nAt Azul, we have developed
  a high-performance JVM, based on OpenJDK, but with significant internal changes
  to deliver both lower latency and higher throughput.  Part of this is replacing
  the legacy C2 JIT compiler with one based on the open-source LLVM project.\r\n\r\nIn
  this session, we'll explore how we've been investigating (and making use of) optimisations
  identified and developed with AI tools such as Claude.  Although this has provided
  some interesting enhancements and overall speed improvements, we'll also discuss
  AI hallucinations claiming improvements that don't exist.\r\n\r\nAt the end, you'll
  have a clearer picture of how AI can be applied to less conventional areas of software
  development."
