What is Devnexus?

Devnexus brings together AI, Java, and modern software development at America’s longest-running developer community conferences. Founded by the Atlanta Java Users Group in 2004, it draws 1500+ developers for hands-on training, expert-led sessions, mentoring, and live demos. With 120+ sessions covering AI-assisted development, AI applications and agents, production engineering, Java, security, and leadership, Devnexus helps developers navigate how AI is changing software while strengthening their core engineering skills.
