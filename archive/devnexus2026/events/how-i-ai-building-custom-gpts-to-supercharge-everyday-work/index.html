How I AI: Building Custom GPTs to Supercharge Everyday Work

AI isn’t just for coding—it’s a force multiplier for every role. In this talk, I’ll show how I use AI daily to automate work, analyze complex projects, and tailor content—without being a traditional developer.

From writing automation scripts in Google Apps Script and Excel, to analyzing R&D projects for tax credit eligibility, to summarizing dense articles into concise insights, AI has become my on-demand assistant.

I’ll also share how I built custom GPTs that review my presentations based on audience personas, identifying gaps in tone, content, and clarity—turning a standard deck into a laser-focused message.

This session is for anyone who wants to move past the AI hype and see concrete, repeatable workflows. You’ll leave with ideas, prompts, and frameworks you can apply tomorrow.
